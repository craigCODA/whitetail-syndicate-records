## How the work was done and used

Prospective story contributors, clubs, organizations, outfitters and partners were researched. Invitations were personalized to their actual programs or experiences. Exact sent messages and replies were recorded, backgrounds compiled, and call preparation developed. Correspondence was transitioned toward info@whitetailsyndicate.com.

Craig reports 139 outreach emails and 38 ongoing conversations in his recap. The later imported file contains 157 sent-message records; its reply check records 27 organization groups and 35 reply messages, using an earlier reply-check timestamp. These are different dated measures. Both are preserved in records/metrics.json; neither is silently substituted for the other.
