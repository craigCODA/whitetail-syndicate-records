# Evidence standards

Source: Craig Ramos's October 10, 2026 instructions for this repository. These standards govern how agents describe the existing records.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use confirmed operator statement > dated original artifact > retrospective estimate > inference for statements about recorded work and billing. Evaluate evidence against the specific claim: a confirmed itemization supports billing amounts and task splits; a dated artifact supports the work product it contains. Neither supplies an unrecorded deployment, permission or owner decision.

Preserve sources that disagree, identify the conflict and leave an unresolved decision open. The hierarchy does not authorize editing original artifacts to match a later statement.

| Evidence class | What it supports | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed operator statement or itemization | Craig's stated work, billing amount, payment status or agreed task split | Does not create an independent time log or an attached work product |
| Dated original artifact | The dated content, correspondence or work product actually present | A proposal or local preview does not establish approval or live deployment |
| Reported, materials not yet attached | Work Craig reports, with the missing source material identified | Reported completion and attached material remain distinct states |
| Retrospective estimate | Approximate effort described after the work | Never convert it into a billable amount |
| Inference | An explicitly labeled interpretation of available evidence | Never record it as confirmed work, payment, approval or publication |
| Open or missing | Evidence or a decision still needed to resolve a claim | Absence is not proof that the work did not occur |

## Rules for recording and answering

- Never convert an approximate effort estimate into a billable amount, even if an agent can multiply it by the hourly rate. Use explicitly confirmed billing inputs.
- Keep “reported by Craig” and “materials not yet attached” visible together when both apply.
- A local preview, draft or reply does not establish live deployment, owner approval or payment. Record each outcome only from evidence for that outcome.
- Interview templates do not establish signed permission, completed recordings or an attached second interview.
- Give each count, balance and status its date and definition. Name later confirmed entries separately from an opening balance.
- Use neutral descriptions of what the records show and what they do not yet contain. Do not praise or defend the documentation.
