## How it has been used

The preparation materials support factual interviews and record the participant's sharing preferences. Craig reports conducting two interviews and completing Nathan's real local hunt story for the live website.

## Continue the process

Create a folder per actual participant. Preserve raw notes or recordings, prepare a transcript, attach factual corrections and sharing records, keep story versions, and link the approved publication. Recording and publication permissions are recorded separately in the existing packet. Blank forms remain templates.

Record a supplied file's date and source. When an interview is completed, add its real identity/date to index.json and register the files. When a story is published, record the exact URL and evidence of that publication.
