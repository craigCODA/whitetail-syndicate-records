PRICE PRINCIPLES
Use Shopify's recorded cost per item as a starting reference, not proof that all printing, supplier fees or handling costs are
included.
Keep a short list of attractive category prices, but do not force a costly supplier item into an inflated consumer price.
For an additional front, back or sleeve imprint, confirm the real incremental charge before approving any upsell; each method
and supplier can differ.
Where a supplier's production cost makes a basic item too expensive, re-source, simplify, place in a true premium tier, or
remove it.
Charge shipping at checkout, then test the actual amount collected against supplier shipping, especially for mixed-provider
orders.
