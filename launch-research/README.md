# Launch research and planning records

Research considered established hunting/apparel brands and related organizations, suppliers, garment choices, pricing, fulfillment, product selection and a community built around real hunting stories.

The findings were turned into written plans and owner-review presentations. [Historical full packet](historical/2026-10-08-full-packet.pdf) and its [readable extraction](historical/2026-10-08-full-packet-readable.md) preserve the earlier proposals. A staged-site presentation is also preserved.

The five-design decision remained unsettled while Kraig and Lori continued creating products. Supplier research, proposed prices, operating plans and draft presentations are work products; they are not evidence that samples, final prices, launch gates or supplier operations were implemented.

Promotion and film development are omitted from the current completed-work recap following Craig's request. Earlier original documents may still contain historical proposals in those areas.

When new research is surfaced, keep its source, retrieval date and relevance to a specific decision. When an owner decides, record the decision and link it to the evidence instead of changing a proposal into an approval without a record.
